# Changelog

## 0.4.0 - 2026-11-20

### New
- Buff buttons beside party frames work with any party frame addon (ElvUI, Cell, Grid2, VuhDo, Shadowed Unit Frames...).
- The settings window shows the addon version next to the title.

### Changed
- Shorter hints in the settings.
- Mana Ticks and Buffs sit under your class's name instead of "Caster"; buff switches have short names (Blessings, Auras...).
- My extra buffs grows and shrinks with the buffs you add.

### Fixed
- The buff reminder now really buffs nearby players outside your group.
- Buff buttons beside party frames no longer show on top of the talent, mail and other windows.

## 0.3.1 - 2026-11-20

### Changed
- Nearby allies: your party and raid members stand out with a blue row (soft gold in the Classic theme).

### Fixed
- Nearby allies: clicking a row targets the player again, and right-click casts your buff on them (Forever's two-word names weren't matched).

## 0.3.0 - 2026-11-19

### New
- Shaman: weapon imbue bar (Rockbiter, Flametongue, Frostbrand, Windfury) with reminders before it runs out.
- Shaman: totem bar with each element's time left, an alert when a totem is killed, right-click to destroy, and Lightning Shield charges.
- Shaman: totem sets (Solo, Group, PvP) dropped one totem per click, and a warning when a totem item is missing from your bags.
- Shaman: cooldown icons for shocks, Grounding Totem, Stormstrike, Nature's Swiftness, Reincarnation and more.

## 0.2.0 - 2026-11-19

### New
- Classic WoW theme: pick "Classic WoW" from the Theme menu at the top of the settings for the game's own frames, gold text and red buttons. Modern stays the default.
- Friendly Names can hide friendly players in cities and inns.
- New Tinker's Belt icon in the AddOns list and a new minimap button.

### Fixed
- Newly learned poisons now show up in the "Add poison" list.

## 0.1.0 - 2026-11-17

First release of Tinker's Belt. Type `/tb` in game to open the settings.

### What's in it

**For everyone**
- PvP Kills: your score against each enemy player, with recent kills and deaths.
- PvP Settings: score in tooltips, kill announcements and sounds for open-world PvP.
- Nearby Enemies: a list of opposite-faction players seen around you.
- Friendly Names: clean labels above friendly players (class colour and guild) instead of nameplates.
- Profiles: save and load action bars and talents, shared by your characters of the same class.
- Auto Restock: buys reagents and supplies at vendors, within a gold limit you set.
- Movable panels: unlock to drag any panel, with a grid, live snapping to edges and centres, and adjustable grid size.

**Casters**
- Mana Ticks: a bar for the five-second rule, then regeneration ticks.
- Buffs: never forget a buff. Reminder buttons for you, your group and nearby players, plus buff buttons beside party frames.

**Rogues**
- Stealth Action Bars: choose which bars switch page while you're in Stealth.
- Poisons: weapon poison bar, reminders before poisons run out, and poisons kept stocked in your bags.
- Combat Bars: health, energy and combo point bars, a Slice and Dice timer that also works in combat, and cooldown icons with adjustable icon and text size.
- Lockboxes: one button that picks and opens your lockboxes.
- Openers: Sap and Pick Pocket icons above enemy nameplates while stealthed, and two weapon set buttons (opener and fight).
