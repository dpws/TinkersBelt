# Changelog

## 0.4.7 - 2026-11-21

### New
- Language menu at the top of the settings: follow your game client, or pick any of the 11 languages.

### Changed
- Buttons and the macro builder's switches fit longer translations instead of spilling over.
- Classic theme: the settings window has a solid background.
- Korean and Chinese text uses a font that has their letters.

### Fixed
- Buffs on low-level players: the highest rank their level allows is cast (nearby allies, buff reminder, party frame buttons).
- Small buttons (the + invite button) stay inside their window and are centred.

## 0.4.6 - 2026-11-21

### New
- Tinker's Belt speaks your language: German, French, Spanish (Spain and Latin America), Italian, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Korean and Chinese (Simplified and Traditional). It follows your game client; anything not yet translated shows in English.

## 0.4.5 - 2026-11-21

### New
- Key bindings for opening the settings, unlocking panels, casting the first missing buff, poisons and imbues, rogue weapon sets, poison crafting and lockboxes.
- A Key bindings button in the settings sidebar (under Unlock panels) opens the Tinker's Belt key bindings.

### Changed
- All key bindings are in their own Tinker's Belt category, each named after its group (General, Weapons, Rogue, Shaman).

## 0.4.4 - 2026-11-21

### New
- Nearby allies: choose which buff right-click casts (Friendly Names page).
- Shamans: key bindings for the totem set and each element's totem (Key Bindings > Tinker's Belt).

### Changed
- PvP Kills and PvP Settings are one PvP page: score, recent kills, then settings.

## 0.4.3 - 2026-11-21

### New
- Shamans: Shift+click a totem button to choose that element's totem for your active set; the tooltip lists what each click does.

## 0.4.2 - 2026-11-21

### New
- Bag Cleaner: when your bags are full, a panel lists the cheapest stacks. Click one to delete it, right-click to protect that item for good. Items with no vendor price are never listed.
- Tooltips on the macro builder's small buttons and switches, the allies invite button and the settings sidebar switches.

## 0.4.1 - 2026-11-21

### New
- Class macros in the macro builder: a curated set for every class (Class macros menu), in your client's language.
- Every feature has an on/off switch in the settings sidebar.
- Poison Stock buys ingredients only as full sets per poison (on by default; switch on the Poison Stock page).

### Changed
- Snapped panels stay on their grid line when they grow, shrink or change size.
- Mana Ticks is now Mana Regen (Forever has no mana ticks).
- Profiles is hidden for now; your saved profiles are kept.

## 0.4.0 - 2026-11-21

### New
- Macro builder (/tb > Macros): build macros from blocks and switches with a live preview, start from templates, pick spells from your spellbook or items from your bags, and open your existing macros as blocks.
- Rogues: a Sap timer above sapped enemies' nameplates.
- PvP Kills: records which of your characters killed or died, with per-character scores, a character filter and a Character column.

### Changed
- Nearby allies: "Hide the list in cities and inns" hides the list (name labels always show).
- Timers count down to 0 like the game's own timers.

## 0.3.2 - 2026-11-20

### New
- Buff buttons beside party frames work with any party frame addon (ElvUI, Cell, Grid2, VuhDo, Shadowed Unit Frames...).
- The settings window shows the addon version next to the title.

### Changed
- Shorter hints in the settings.
- Mana Ticks and Buffs sit under your class's name instead of "Caster"; buff switches have short names (Blessings, Auras...).
- My extra buffs grows and shrinks with the buffs you add.

### Fixed
- The buff reminder now really buffs nearby players outside your group.
- Buff buttons beside party frames no longer show on top of the talent, mail and other windows.

## 0.3.1 - 2026-11-20

### Changed
- Nearby allies: your party and raid members stand out with a blue row (soft gold in the Classic theme).

### Fixed
- Nearby allies: clicking a row targets the player again, and right-click casts your buff on them (Forever's two-word names weren't matched).

## 0.3.0 - 2026-11-19

### New
- Shaman: weapon imbue bar (Rockbiter, Flametongue, Frostbrand, Windfury) with reminders before it runs out.
- Shaman: totem bar with each element's time left, an alert when a totem is killed, right-click to destroy, and Lightning Shield charges.
- Shaman: totem sets (Solo, Group, PvP) dropped one totem per click, and a warning when a totem item is missing from your bags.

## 0.2.0 - 2026-11-19

### Fixed

## 0.1.0 - 2026-11-17

First release of Tinker's Belt. Type `/tb` in game to open the settings.

### What's in it

**For everyone**
- PvP Kills: your score against each enemy player, with recent kills and deaths.
- PvP Settings: score in tooltips, kill announcements and sounds for open-world PvP.
- Nearby Enemies: a list of opposite-faction players seen around you.
- Friendly Names: clean labels above friendly players (class colour and guild) instead of nameplates.
- Profiles: save and load action bars and talents, shared by your characters of the same class.
- Auto Restock: buys reagents and supplies at vendors, within a gold limit you set.
- Movable panels: unlock to drag any panel, with a grid, live snapping to edges and centres, and adjustable grid size.

**Casters**
- Mana Ticks: a bar for the five-second rule, then regeneration ticks.
- Buffs: never forget a buff. Reminder buttons for you, your group and nearby players, plus buff buttons beside party frames.

**Rogues**
- Stealth Action Bars: choose which bars switch page while you're in Stealth.
- Poisons: weapon poison bar, reminders before poisons run out, and poisons kept stocked in your bags.
- Combat Bars: health, energy and combo point bars, a Slice and Dice timer that also works in combat, and cooldown icons with adjustable icon and text size.
- Lockboxes: one button that picks and opens your lockboxes.
- Openers: Sap and Pick Pocket icons above enemy nameplates while stealthed, and two weapon set buttons (opener and fight).
