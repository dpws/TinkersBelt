# Tinker's Belt

Small quality-of-life tools for **World of Warcraft: Forever**. Type `/tb` in game to open the settings.

## For everyone
- **Buff reminders**: buttons for buffs missing on you, your group and nearby players; buff buttons beside party frames (Blizzard, DandersFrames, ElvUI, Cell, Grid2 and others).
- **Macro builder**: build macros from blocks and switches with a live preview, start from templates or a curated set for your class, pick spells from your spellbook, and open your existing macros as blocks.
- **Movable panels**: unlock to drag any panel, with a grid and live snapping; snapped panels stay on their line when they change size.
- **Feature switches**: turn each tool on or off right in the settings list.
- **PvP**: your score against each enemy player, per character and across all your characters, kill announcements and sounds, and a list of nearby enemies.
- **Friendly names**: clean class-coloured labels instead of nameplates, plus a clickable nearby allies list (right-click casts the buff you choose).
- **Auto restock**: buys reagents at vendors within a gold limit.
- **Bag Cleaner**: when your bags are full, lists the cheapest stacks to delete with a click; right-click protects an item.
- **Themes**: Modern, or Classic WoW frames.
- **Key bindings**: keys for buffs, poisons and imbues, weapon sets, lockboxes, totems and more (Key Bindings > Tinker's Belt).

## Rogues
- Stealth action bar paging
- Poison bar with reminders, and poisons kept stocked and crafted
- Combo, energy and Slice and Dice bars, cooldown icons
- One-click lockbox opening
- Sap / Pick Pocket icons, a Sap timer on sapped enemies, and opener weapon sets

## Shamans
- Weapon imbue bar with reminders
- Totem bar: timers, kill alerts, right-click to destroy, Lightning Shield charges
- Totem sets (Solo / Group / PvP), one totem per click; Shift+click a totem button to choose its totem; key bindings for the set and each element
- Cooldown icons (shocks, Grounding, Stormstrike...)

## Install
Install with the CurseForge app, or extract the zip into `World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns\` so you get `AddOns\TinkersBelt\TinkersBelt.toc`, then restart the game.
