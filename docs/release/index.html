How to install

  1. Close the game. A new addon folder is only picked up when the game starts; later updates just need /reload.

  2. Download the zip above.

  3. If an older version is installed, delete its folder first: Interface\AddOns\TinkersBelt

  4. Extract the zip into your game's AddOns folder, so the addon ends up here (not in a doubled TinkersBelt\TinkersBelt): World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns\TinkersBelt\TinkersBelt.toc

  5. Start the game and make sure "Tinker's Belt" is ticked in the AddOns list on the character screen. If it says out of date, tick "Load out of date AddOns".

  6. In game, type /tb to open the settings. Your settings carry over between versions.

0.1.017 Nov 2026 · first release

For everyone

  • PvP Kills: your score against each enemy player, with recent kills and deaths.
  • PvP Settings: score in tooltips, kill announcements and sounds for open-world PvP.
  • Nearby Enemies: a list of opposite-faction players seen around you.
  • Friendly Names: clean labels above friendly players (class colour and guild) instead of nameplates.
  • Profiles: save and load action bars and talents, shared by your characters of the same class.
  • Auto Restock: buys reagents and supplies at vendors, within a gold limit you set.
  • Movable panels: unlock to drag any panel, with a grid, live snapping to edges and centres, and adjustable grid size.

Casters

  • Mana Ticks: a bar for the five-second rule, then regeneration ticks.
  • Buffs: reminder buttons for you, your group and nearby players, plus buff buttons beside party frames.

Rogues

  • Stealth Action Bars: choose which bars switch page while you're in Stealth.
  • Poisons: weapon poison bar, reminders before poisons run out, and poisons kept stocked in your bags.
  • Combat Bars: health, energy and combo point bars, a Slice and Dice timer that also works in combat, and cooldown icons with adjustable icon and text size.
  • Lockboxes: one button that picks and opens your lockboxes.
  • Openers: Sap and Pick Pocket icons above enemy nameplates while stealthed, and two weapon set buttons (opener and fight).
